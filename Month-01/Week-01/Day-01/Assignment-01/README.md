# Assignment 01 — Basic Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**Organization:** BrightLayer Stores (simulated business)  
**Business size:** 8 employees  
**Assessment type:** Basic cybersecurity risk assessment

## 1. Five Important Assets

1. **Employee email accounts:** Used for business communication and may provide access to password-reset links and other company services.
2. **Shared computers:** Employees use these devices to perform daily work. If compromised, they could expose business information or allow unauthorized access to other accounts.
3. **Company website:** Represents the business online and may be important for customer communication, reputation, and sales.
4. **Cloud storage containing invoices:** Holds business and potentially customer-related financial records. Unauthorized access, deletion, or disclosure could disrupt operations.
5. **User passwords:** Protect access to company accounts and systems. If stolen or reused, they could allow an attacker to access multiple services.

## 2. Three Potential Threats

1. **Phishing attacker:** Sends deceptive emails to trick employees into revealing credentials or opening malicious attachments.
2. **Cybercriminal targeting internet-facing services:** Attempts to exploit weaknesses in the company website to access information or disrupt availability.
3. **Unauthorized user on public Wi-Fi:** A nearby attacker attempts to intercept poorly protected traffic or access devices and services through an insecure network.

## 3. Three Vulnerabilities

1. **Weak or reused passwords:** If employees use easily guessed or repeated passwords, one compromised credential may expose multiple accounts. This weakness needs to be verified.
2. **Unpatched website software:** Outdated website components may contain known security flaws that attackers can exploit. The software's patch status must be checked.
3. **Inadequate Wi-Fi security or network separation:** Weak wireless authentication or a lack of separation between guest and business devices could increase the chance of unauthorized access. The router configuration must be reviewed.

## 4. Associated Risks

- **Risk 1 — Email and cloud account compromise:** A phishing attacker exploits weak or reused passwords to access an employee account. The attacker may then access invoices or other business information, resulting in data exposure, financial loss, and reputational damage.

- **Risk 2 — Website compromise:** A cybercriminal exploits unpatched website software to gain unauthorized access or disrupt the site. This could cause downtime, expose information, and reduce customer trust.

- **Risk 3 — Unauthorized network access:** An attacker exploits inadequate Wi-Fi security or network separation to reach shared computers or other internal resources. This could expose business data or provide a starting point for further attacks.

These are plausible scenarios, not confirmed incidents or vulnerabilities.

## 5. Security Recommendations

1. **Reduce credential risk:** Require unique, strong passwords, use a password manager, enable multifactor authentication, and train employees to recognize phishing. This reduces Risk 1.
2. **Improve email protection:** Enable available spam and phishing filters, establish a process for reporting suspicious messages, and provide regular awareness training. This also reduces Risk 1.
3. **Maintain the website:** Inventory website software, apply security updates promptly, remove unnecessary components, and review access permissions. This reduces Risk 2.
4. **Secure the Wi-Fi network:** Use modern wireless encryption, change default administrator credentials, update router firmware, and separate guest Wi-Fi from business devices. This reduces Risk 3.
5. **Protect important files:** Restrict cloud-storage access by job role, maintain protected backups of invoices, and periodically test file recovery. This limits the impact of data loss or account compromise across the identified risks.

The business should first verify the suspected weaknesses, prioritize the risks based on likelihood and impact, and then track the recommended improvements.
